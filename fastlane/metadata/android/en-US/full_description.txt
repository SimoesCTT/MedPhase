A phase-domain processing tool for DICOM medical imaging data.

Loads DICOM files, extracts pixel data, applies a choice of denoising
filters, and reports PSNR against the original. All processing runs on
device; the app requests no network permission.

Features:
* Load DICOM files (Explicit VR LE and Implicit VR LE)
* 8-bit, 16-bit, and 32-bit integer pixel data
* Window/level or min-max normalization
* Denoising methods:
  - Gaussian blur
  - Median 3x3
  - Non-local means
  - Riemann-zero comb filter
* PSNR comparison against the original

Status: research and educational tool. Not a medical device. Does not
diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.
